## Generated for this project

- `assets/audio/radio_voice.wav`: the radio line, spoken by the Windows "Microsoft David Desktop" text-to-speech voice and generated on this machine. Check Microsoft's terms for that voice before distributing the game commercially, or replace the file with a recorded voice.
- Wind and the low drone are synthesized live in `intro/audio.js`.
- Fire, smoke, ash, blood, muzzle flash, sky and the title's metal treatment are drawn by code in `intro/`.
